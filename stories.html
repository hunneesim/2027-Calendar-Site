Desk Calendar 2027 · Instagram

Seven graphics, two sizes

Stories are 1080 × 1920. Posts are 1080 × 1350, the tallest a feed post can be. Post them in this order: as a carousel in the feed, or one after another in your story.

On a phone: "Save all" opens the share sheet; choose Save Images to put them in Photos. Without a share sheet, the files download instead.

Stories: the space at the bottom of each one is left clear for the link sticker, under "Tap the link to order".

Posts: feed captions can't hold a link, so these say "Link in bio". Put the order page in your bio while orders are open.
